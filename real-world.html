Part 1

Real World Data: Target Trial Emulation & Digital Twins Example

Target trial emulation designs an analysis of observational data to mirror the protocol of the randomised trial you would ideally run: eligibility, treatment strategies, start of follow-up and outcomes. A digital twin is a data-driven virtual counterpart of a patient, used to predict how that patient might respond under different treatments.
